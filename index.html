About the model & data

Simulated car positions are seeded from live TomTom traffic observations and then evolve independently. They are estimates, not live tracking of individual vehicles. Population is a modelling choice. Traffic light locations come from OpenStreetMap; timing is simulated. Turn restrictions and conflicts at unsignalised junctions are not modelled. Epicenters highlight cells with at least 8 cars where at least half travel below 11 km/h; signals can also cause these queues.
