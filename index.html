Traffic lights: simulated signals (zoom in to see)

Simulated car positions are seeded from live TomTom traffic observations and then evolve independently. They are estimates, not live tracking of individual vehicles.

About this simulation

Pause and re-initialize apply to everyone viewing this server. The car count is a modelling choice, not a measured London count. Traffic light locations come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors); their timing is simulated (fixed 90 s cycles), because live signal states are not available. There are no turn restrictions, and junctions without signals behave as simple merges. Changing the slider only affects the next re-initialization, which moves the same cars to positions weighted by live TomTom traffic.
